# Multi-Omics Analysis of Gene Expression and Alternative Splicing in Clear Cell Renal Cell Carcinoma (KIRC)

## Overview

This project investigates transcriptomic alterations associated with tumour progression in clear cell renal cell carcinoma (KIRC) through the integrated analysis of gene expression and alternative splicing.

The study was developed as part of a Master's Thesis in Bioinformatics at the International University of La Rioja (UNIR), using publicly available data from TCGA and TCGA-SpliceSeq.

## Objectives

- Identify gene expression alterations associated with tumour progression.
- Characterize differential alternative splicing events across tumour stages.
- Integrate gene expression and alternative splicing results to identify candidate genes.
- Explore the prognostic relevance of candidate genes through survival analysis.

## Data

- **Cohort:** 530 patients with KIRC.
- **Gene expression:** TCGA RNA-seq data.
- **Alternative splicing:** TCGA-SpliceSeq PSI data.
- **Clinical information:** TCGA clinical data.

## Methods

The analysis included:

- Cohort selection and data integration.
- Exploratory data analysis and quality assessment.
- Differential gene expression analysis.
- Differential alternative splicing analysis.
- Splicing event annotation.
- Multi-omics integration.
- Functional enrichment analysis.
- Tumour status association analysis.
- Overall survival analysis.

## Key Findings

The integration of differential gene expression and alternative splicing analyses identified six candidate genes showing alterations at both transcriptomic levels.

Among them, **SAA2** emerged as the most consistent candidate, displaying coordinated expression and alternative splicing alterations associated with tumour progression and overall survival.
